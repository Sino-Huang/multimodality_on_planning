   'understated' is per instance: true_peak / trace-visible peak, max and mean
   over the instrumented runs at that object count.

   seen_feature_delta is computed as the difference of two CLIPPED snapshots
   (cgas_certificate_contracts.py:41-43), so at width 2 it is unsound by the
   understatement factor above. Emitting the delta removes the clip from the
   correctness path entirely -- the delta is bounded by novelty_items(state, 2),
   which is |atoms| + C(|atoms|, 2) and does not grow with the search.
