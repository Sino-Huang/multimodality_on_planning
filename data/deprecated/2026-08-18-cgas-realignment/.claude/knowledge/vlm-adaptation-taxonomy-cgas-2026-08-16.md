# VLM adaptation taxonomy and CGAS positioning

**Question.** Does direct VLM action-plus-certificate training and later Certificate-Guided Adaptive Scaffolding (CGAS) fit zero-shot, in-context learning (ICL), supervised fine-tuning (SFT), or Experience Distillation (EPD)?

## Source-backed taxonomy

- **Zero-shot (ZS):** inference with no target-task gradient update and no demonstrations. Brown et al. distinguish zero-/one-/few-shot by the number of demonstrations at inference, while all remain parameter-fixed for the task ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.14165)).
- **ICL:** parameter-fixed inference conditioned on demonstrations or task-specific evidence in the prompt/context. The EPD paper treats task-specific interaction history as such evidence, so its benefit disappears when that context is removed ([EPD §1–2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21051)); it is not synonymous with merely using a long context or external memory.
- **SFT:** parameter updates on target outputs. In the EPD comparison, direct SFT trains on collected trajectories and is evaluated without the experience context; unlike EPD it does not first generate targets from an experience-conditioned teacher ([EPD §4.1–4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21051)).
- **Context/experience distillation:** a privileged-context teacher produces targets and a student is trained without that context to reproduce them. The general context-distillation construction explicitly has a richer teacher template, a poorer student template, and an extracted teacher target ([Klein & Zhong §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.15189)). EPD makes the privileged context a self-collected interaction trajectory, samples a teacher next action at already recorded histories, and avoids further environment interaction ([EPD §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.21051)).

## Classification of this project

1. **Direct action-plus-certificate baseline = joint/multi-task SFT (offline behavioral cloning), not EPD.** It trains one VLM to predict the expert action and typed certificate from the fixed observation; the planned loss is `L_action + λ_c L_certificate` ([proposal §2](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L20-L38)), and Phase 3 calls it a “direct VLM action-plus-certificate baseline” ([execution plan §Phase 3](../high_level_plans/research_execution_plan.md#L296-L311)). The targets come from planner code/expert transitions, including valid certificates and one-invariant counterfactuals ([proposal §5.2](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L99-L108)), not an experience-conditioned teacher’s resampled actions. Calling it “experience distillation” would be incorrect.

2. **Direct decoding at CGAS inference = ZS only if it has neither prior certificate/memory nor task-specific demonstrations.** This is the `direct` (cost zero) condition, which predicts from the fixed VLA observation ([proposal §2](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L24-L38)). It is a deployment condition/baseline, not a training method; call it **direct/no-support inference** when avoiding ambiguity about pretraining and instruction context.

3. **The certificate and memory scaffolds are runtime structured support, not automatically ICL.** Prior verifier-approved certificates and state-keyed-memory results are supplied during inference ([proposal §2](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L24-L38); [proposal §5.3](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L110-L112)). They are best named **certificate-context conditioning** and **external-memory/tool conditioning**. They become an ICL-like condition only if a controlled context actually contains demonstration/experience records whose conditioning improves behavior with frozen weights. Retrieval alone is not evidence of ICL.

4. **CGAS legitimately composes SFT with adaptive inference-time support, and extends the cited taxonomy at the system level.** Its distinct proposed element is not a new parameter-adaptation paradigm: it is a learned, cost-aware controller trained with verifier-derived, counterfactual minimum-cost scaffold labels ([proposal §3.2](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L54-L60); [proposal §5.2](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L99-L108)). The execution plan preserves identical raw VLA observation, context budget, examples, and action vocabulary across paths ([execution plan §Phase 4](../high_level_plans/research_execution_plan.md#L406-L423)). This supports the narrower claim: **SFT-trained joint prediction plus verifier-supervised adaptive context/memory allocation**, rather than “a new ICL/EPD method.”

## Terminology corrections

- Use **typed symbolic search certificate**, not “rationale” or chain-of-thought: the proposal explicitly says the certificate is a compact target, not a natural-language explanation ([proposal §5.1](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L84-L97)).
- Use **joint action-and-certificate SFT** (or *offline behavior cloning with auxiliary certificate supervision*) for the direct model; reserve **experience distillation** for a teacher-with-experience / student-without-experience objective.
- Use **adaptive scaffolding/runtime support allocation** for CGAS. Avoid calling its controller “adaptive computation” unless it changes computation depth/halting; it chooses bounded information/tool support.
- Do not equate **memory** with **ICL**, nor infer ICL from any certificate context. State the actual conditioning material and whether model weights remain fixed.

## Experimental implications

1. **Separate factors and name arms precisely.** Cross `training target` (action-only SFT; joint SFT; optional actual EPD) with `inference support` (direct; prior-certificate context; live memory; CGAS). This avoids attributing gains from certificate SFT to ICL/memory or vice versa.
2. **Keep the already-proposed matched-support controls.** Hold observation, backbone, context budget, action vocabulary, examples, storage interface, and support budget constant; compare direct, always-certificate, always-memory, confidence router, and CGAS as specified ([proposal §6.1–6.3](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L126-L160)). Report verified certificate fidelity, valid-plan success, support cost, and route optimality—not final success alone.
3. **Add an ICL claim only with its required counterfactual.** For frozen weights, compare no task-specific certificate/experience context against an explicitly serialized prior-certificate or trajectory context at equal token budget; evaluate after removing it. Otherwise report conditioning/tool use, not ICL.
4. **Add EPD only as a clearly separate extension.** Collect failed/successful planning episodes; condition a fixed teacher on a compressed, verifier-approved episode/certificate history; generate action-plus-certificate targets at recorded states; train a student without that history; charge no new planning-environment interactions after collection. Compare against direct SFT on the identical collected episodes, plus teacher-with-context and ZS student. This implements the EPD distinction rather than relabeling current expert SFT.
5. **Protect causal interpretation of route labels.** Freeze the schema, palette, measured costs, and counterfactual generator after calibration as planned ([proposal §5.4](../../docs/research_proposal.md#L114-L122)); use one-invariant variants and no-oracle-leakage memory records. The hard calibration gate is appropriate: without recurrent, certificate-localized failures, CGAS has no justified adaptive-scaffolding claim ([execution plan §Phase 3](../high_level_plans/research_execution_plan.md#L352-L358)).

## Conclusion

The present proposal is correctly positioned as **joint SFT plus verifier-supervised adaptive runtime scaffolding**. It composes familiar parameter training and context/tool conditioning, while its potential novelty lies in executable certificate invariants and minimum-cost counterfactual routing labels. It is **not** EPD and should not make an ICL claim without an explicit frozen-weight, task-specific-context ablation. A future EPD arm is feasible, but is a new data-generation/training protocol rather than a rename of the current one.
