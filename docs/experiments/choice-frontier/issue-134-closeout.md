## R1 — submission-order-serial identity counterfactual (finding 49)

**Artifact:** `outputs/native-arms/v1/identity-audit-submission-order.json`
(schema `native_arms_identity_audit_submission_order_v1`),
sha256 `52e1e32c09ade4ad5e644cb74982139327def3f4d7e3f21ebbf17555111ec520`,
generated by `scripts/run_identity_audit_submission_order.py`.

The same 48 additive enumeration-contract pairs as
`outputs/native-arms/v1/identity-audit.json` (24 tasks x {best_first_add_greedy, best_first_add_w3})
were re-run under both serial rules with the frozen control policies (exact_reference = candidate
row 0; random_valid = uniform row, `random.Random(17)`, the frozen baseline evaluation seed) and the
frozen caps (2x reference decisions, reference expansions).

**Validation gates (both passed, fail-run-on-mismatch):**

- Every executed-rule re-run reproduces its stored baseline episode exactly: 288 stored episodes
  checked on decision count, expansion count, goal flag, termination and invalid count (48 pairs x
  2 conditions x 3 modalities), plus the full `raw_output` sequence for all 96 text-state episodes
  (`validation.stored_episode_counts_checked`, `validation.stored_raw_output_sequences_checked`,
  `validation.mismatches: []`).
- exact_reference submits in candidate order, so it is provably rule-invariant; its trajectory is
  identical under both serial rules on all 48 pairs
  (`validation.exact_reference_rule_invariance`: 48/48).

**Result — the killer test returns opposite verdicts on the two rules:**

| serial rule | pairs identical | pairs divergent |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `sorted_candidate_order` (executed) | **48** | 0 |
| `submission_order` (counterfactual variant) | 29 | **19** |

Under the variant, random_valid and exact_reference diverge on at least one decision in 19/48 pairs
(10/24 tasks x both algorithm cells, except storage-expanded greedy-only). First-divergence decision
indices range 2–50 (median 6); per-pair values are in
`pairs[].rules.submission_order.first_divergence_decision_index`. Two pairs flip the goal outcome:
on ferry-expanded-915109 (both algorithms) exact_reference still solves while random_valid
exhausts the expansion budget — under the executed rule all four episodes are decision- and
expansion-identical.

**Pre-registered prediction: not held as formalized.** `preregistered_prediction.held` is `false`:
the executed-rule half held (48/48 identical), but the variant diverged in 19/48 pairs, below the
pre-registered "most pairs" (> 24/48) threshold. The qualitative App B mechanism is confirmed —
the 48/48 identity is a property of the executed serial rule (pre-submission serials from the
sorted candidate order), not of the enumeration contract alone — but the effect is weaker than
predicted: on 29/48 pairs every equal-priority tie happens to break identically under both
assignment orders over the whole trajectory.
