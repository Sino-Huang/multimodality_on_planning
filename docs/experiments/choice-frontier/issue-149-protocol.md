## 1. Questions

- **Q1 (size).** Do the frozen node-choice adapters generalise to instances larger than the
  training generator's size range?
- **Q2 (targets).** Can a node-choice policy use fewer expansions than GBFS(h_add), the heuristic
  its first teacher imitated, when it is trained on a better target: (a) h\*, or (b) its own
  fewest-expansion solved episodes (expert iteration)?
- **Q3 (baselines).** How do learned heuristics on the same 128 px scenes, driving GBFS through the
  same runtime, compare?

## 2. Conventions owned by this protocol (adopted by #159)

All quantities are computed from the trusted episode events (the #133/#135 reconstruction).

- **R_t** = expansions of the uncapped `exact_reference` node-choice episode of the task and
  algorithm (κ head always selected, goal test at selection, no overflow rule). For
  `best_first_add_greedy`, R_t is the expansion count of **GBFS(h_add)** on the task.
- **Expansions-to-goal** of a solved episode = expansions charged before the decision that selects a
  goal node (`goal.preceding_expansions`; the goal selection itself is uncharged).
- **Solved at multiplier m** iff the goal decision index (1-based) ≤ ⌊m·R_t⌋ and the expansions-to-goal
  ≤ ⌊m·R_t⌋ (#133). M1 = trapezoid over m ∈ {1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2}, weights
  0.125/0.25/0.25/0.25/0.125. Solved-at-2R = the m = 2 value.
- **Expansion ratio** ρ = expansions-to-goal / R_t (solved episodes only).
- **Restricted expansions** E = expansions-to-goal if solved within 2·R_t, else 2·R_t (unsolved,
  invalid-output, overflow and budget stops). **Relative restricted expansions** e = E / R_t − 1 ∈
  [−1, 1]; negative means fewer expansions than GBFS(h_add) (for w3: than exact WA\*(h_add)).
- **Plan length** = number of actions on the trusted parent chain from s0 to the selected goal node.
  **C\*** = the optimal plan length from Fast Downward A\*(LM-cut) (§5), plan replay-verified through
  `PDDLStateAuthority`. Plan quality: plan length / C\* and plan length / exact-reference plan length
  (solved episodes only; the number of tasks both arms solve is always reported with any paired
  quality contrast).

GBFS(h_add) itself is the uncapped exact greedy episode (R_t; no context limit). Scorer arms have
no context limit and run with no overflow rule. Inference for VLM arms is the #146 block (float32,
`visual_sdpa`, greedy decoding, inference seed 17, single inputs above the 24,000 padded-batch cap
allowed up to 32,384 tokens).

**Identifiability.** h\* is a function of the state and the goal, so the h\* head is identifiable
from a state-only menu in principle, unlike the history-dependent BFS/BFWS κ of #146. The expert
iteration target is a function of the model's own history and is not.

## 5. h\* labels (D2a; shared with #158)

- Fast Downward 24.06+ (git `ba6ffb797`, the `up_fast_downward` 0.5.2 manylinux wheel, GPL-3.0),
  called as a subprocess `fast-downward.py --search astar(lmcut())` with a 600 s search limit. Not
  vendored.
- A state's problem = the task problem with `:init` replaced by the static initial facts plus the
  state's atoms (`expanded_candidates.replace_initial` / `pddl_facts`). The returned plan is replayed
  through `PDDLStateAuthority` from the state and must reach the goal; its length is h\*.
  Unsolvable → h\* = `null` with status `dead_end`; timeout → status `timeout` (counted, reported).
- **Labelled states:** every unique (task, state) in the menus of the v7 additive records (the
  #136 membership, both algorithms, training and diagnostic; 4,769 states), plus h_add
  (`AdditiveHeuristic`) of each.
- Output `configs/experiments/choice-frontier-v8/hstar-labels.json` (per row: task_id, state atoms
  sha, view-catalog index, h\*, status, h_add), **committed before any training**. A 10 % random
  subsample is re-solved by blind breadth-first search where it finishes within 2,000,000 states; any
  disagreement fails the gate.

## 6. h\*-target adapters (D2a)

- **Records:** exactly the v7 `best_first_add_greedy` cell (2,048 training + 16 diagnostic, the
  #136 decisions and menus; v7 store read-only). Only the teacher changes.
- **Teacher:** the menu state with minimum h\*; ties by the frozen κ order (h_add, then generation
  serial) recovered by replaying the #136 teacher from PDDL. `dead_end` states rank last. If every
  menu state is a dead end, the κ head.
- ×2 menu-order augmentation (`run_choice_frontier_v3.augment_menu`), sample order `order:17`, the
  #136/#146 recipe (one epoch, 128 updates, r 64 / α 128, lr 1e-4 cosine, bf16, fp32 adapters, Qwen3-VL-8B
  rev `0c351dd0`), seeds 17/29/71, observations visual and text. Augmentation check ±5 pp.
- Reported: share of records where the h\* teacher differs from the h_add teacher.

## 7. Expert-iteration adapters (D2b)

- **Rollouts:** for each observation (visual, text), the three frozen greedy h_add adapters
  (seeds 17/29/71) run one episode each on every #136 task that contributes greedy records, in the
  #136 task order, under the §4 contract with cap 2·R_t (R_t = that task's uncapped exact greedy
  expansions). Views: the #136 train-views (read-only) plus live renders under `outputs/choice-frontier/v8/`.
- **Selection:** per task and observation, the solved rollout with the fewest expansions-to-goal;
  ties to the lowest seed. Tasks with no solved rollout contribute nothing.
- **Records:** the selected episode's decisions with menu ≥ 2 (goal selection included), labelled
  with the model's own choice; the first 64 per task, tasks walked in #136 order until 2,048
  training records, then 16 diagnostics. If fewer exist, all are used, the count is reported, and
  steps = ceil(2·records / 32).
- Same augmentation, order pin, recipe, seeds and observations as §6. Fresh LoRA from the base model.
- The policy-gradient variant is not run.

## 8. Learned-heuristic driver (D3; shared with #158)

`examples/planning_benchmark_slice/scored_node_choice.py`. A `StateScorer` maps menu states to
scores ŝ. At each decision the driver picks the menu label of the minimum key — ŝ for GBFS,
g + 3·ŝ for WA\* (g = the runtime's path cost of the node) — ties by generation serial, and submits
it through the frozen runtime as arm `scored_heuristic`. The per-menu scores are stored in each
event. Replay: `replay_node_choice_episode` plus a check that every choice is the stored-score
argmin; deterministic scorers are re-scored exactly.

## 9. Image regressors and rankers (D3)

- **Input:** the 128 px unlabelled state scene (resized to 224) and the task's goal page(s)
  (resized to 384 × 512, embeddings averaged over pages). Shared backbone; fusion MLP on
  [z_scene, z_goal, z_scene ⊙ z_goal].
- **Backbones:** CNN = torchvision ResNet-18 `IMAGENET1K_V1`; ViT = timm
  `vit_small_patch16_224.augreg_in21k_ft_in1k` (`dynamic_img_size=True`). Fine-tuned end to end.
- **Models:** regressor on h\* (dead ends excluded from training), regressor on h_add, pairwise
  ranker (logistic loss on within-menu pairs with different h\*), per backbone: 6 models × seeds
  17/29/71.
- **Data:** the labelled corpus states (§5), scenes from the v7 store. Task-grouped split: a fixed
  10 % of corpus tasks (sha256 of task id, lowest decile) is validation for epoch selection only.
  AdamW, lr 1e-4, weight decay 0.01, batch 64, 20 epochs, select the epoch with the lowest validation
  loss. Regression loss: Huber on the raw cost.
- **Driving:** regressors drive GBFS and WA\*; rankers drive GBFS only. Panel scenes are rendered
  through the frozen live-view path.

## 10. Evaluation and replay

- Every arm runs on every task of its strata (§4 table). Controls: `exact_reference` (seed 17) and
  `random_valid` (5 seeds) per (algorithm, observation) under the overflow rule; S0 controls must
  equal the v7 controls (gate).
- **Smoke gate** (#132 definition) for the 4 new adapter cells (h\*, EI × visual, text) at seed 17
  on the three #132 smoke tasks; PASS iff accepted-call rate ≥ 0.5; FAIL gates out the cell.
- **Independent replay** of every episode (VLM, control, scorer, rollout): menus, bindings, token
  counts, trusted transitions, the overflow stop and, for scorer arms, the argmin check. 0 mismatch
  required; missingness is stated.

## 11. Pre-registered analysis

Task-cluster bootstrap, `random.Random(133)` fresh per statistic, 10,000 draws, 95 % percentile
interval; adapter seeds averaged inside each draw, random-valid's 5 seeds averaged.

- **Test A (Q2, primary).** Per method ∈ {h\*-adapter, EI-adapter} × observation ∈ {visual, text} ×
  stratum ∈ {S0, S1, S2}, on GBFS: Δ = mean over tasks of e(method) (seed-mean), i.e. relative
  restricted expansions vs GBFS(h_add). Verdicts in order: **FEWER** (hi < 0), **MORE** (lo > 0),
  **EQUIVALENT** (−0.10 < lo and hi < 0.10; margin = 10 % of R_t), **INCONCLUSIVE**. Holm over these
  12 tests at α = 0.05 using the one-sided bootstrap p (share of draws with Δ ≥ 0), reported as
  `fewer_holm`.
- **Test B (Q2, target contrast).** Δ(method) − Δ(h_add adapter), paired by task, same observation
  and stratum; SEPARATED_BELOW (hi < 0) / SEPARATED_ABOVE (lo > 0) / NOT_SEPARATED.
- **Test C (Q1).** Per algorithm × observation × stratum for the h_add adapters: D3 = M1(learned) −
  M1(random_valid) with the #138 rule (δ = 0.05, equivalence ±0.05: POSITIVE / EQUIVALENT /
  NEGATIVE / INCONCLUSIVE); learned − `gbfs_goal_count` M1 (paired; SEPARATED verdicts); and Δ.
  Size trend (descriptive): D3(S1), D3(S2) − D3(S0) with unpaired task-cluster intervals.
- **Test D (Q3).** For every scorer arm and every VLM arm: M1, solved-at-2R, Δ; paired contrasts
  scorer − visual h_add adapter on M1 and on Δ (SEPARATED verdicts).
- **Descriptive:** solved-vs-budget curves per method × stratum; restricted expansions vs budget;
  plan length / C\*, plan length / exact, ρ; overflow and invalid counts per arm; C\*-bin breakdown;
  h\*-head and h_add-head agreement minus chance; regressor validation error.
- **Interpretation rules.** EQUIVALENT is the only basis for a "matches h_add" claim. FEWER on S0
  but not S1/S2 is reported as in-range only. A text-only FEWER with visual INCONCLUSIVE is reported
  as a perception limit, not a planning result. Overflow-driven failures are reported separately
  from budget failures. Either outcome is a result.

## Amendment A1 (2026-10-01, before any panel, label, control or model episode)

Implementation details fixed while writing the code; no outcome exists.

1. **h_add adapters on S0 are re-run, not reused.** The #139 S0 visual episodes ran under the
   `visual-choice-frontier-v1` contract, which has no overflow rule. To keep every D1/Test A–D
   contrast inside one contract, all h_add adapter cells (both algorithms, both observations, three
   seeds) run on S0 under the §4 node-choice contract (+≈ 3.6 GPU-h, inside the cap). The #139 and
   #146 S0 episodes are compared descriptively as a cross-run reproducibility check.
2. **D3 encoder details.** Each backbone uses its own pretrained normalisation (ImageNet mean/std
   for ResNet-18; mean/std 0.5 from timm's `pretrained_cfg` for the ViT). BatchNorm layers keep
   their pretrained running statistics (eval mode) while their affine weights train, because goal
   pages are encoded in small per-task batches. Batches hold about four tasks (per-task chunks of
   batch/4, shuffled with `random.Random(seed)`). Ranker pairs are unique unordered within-menu
   pairs with different non-null h\*.
3. **EI membership is per observation.** `membership-ei.json` holds one training/diagnostic list
   per observation (the visual and text rollouts select different episodes); its sha covers both.
   Visual EI records bind the rollout's live-rendered scenes; text EI records need none.
4. **Scorer timeouts.** In `gbfs_hstar_oracle`, an FD timeout scores like a dead end (+∞) and is
   counted.
5. **D3 replay.** Image-scorer episodes are verified by independent replay plus the stored-score
   argmin check; they are not re-scored on GPU (floating-point non-determinism). `goal_count` and
   `gbfs_hstar_oracle` episodes are re-scored exactly.
