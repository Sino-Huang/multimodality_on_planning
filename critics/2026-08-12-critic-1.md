## Findings

### CRITICAL — The “remaining gates” estimate suppresses unfinished technical prerequisites to authorization

**Location:** `doc/high_level_plans/research_execution_plan.md:553–571` (Immediate Next Step 8; Step 11).

**Offending text:** “**Exact missing evidence/approval before the production-render unblock:** finalize, review, and gate the adapter/problem-writer canonical `b1..bN` + July formatting patch; passing mapping-bound 8-object AND non-empty-goal 12-object smokes through the selected actual production path...” (lines 553–557). Yet Step 11 calls the remaining gates “(a) explicit owner/operator authorization and completion of the digest-bound resumable 16,822-state render ...; (b) ... alignment ...; (c) ... release ...; and (d) ... configuration...” (lines 565–570), then states: “The authorization gate (a) is resolved by the owner/operator, not by further proof runs” (lines 570–571).

**Why this is blocking:** Step 8 itself says the writer patch and two actual-production-path smokes are *missing before* the production-render unblock. Step 11 then omits them from its exhaustive “remaining gates” list and implies that no further proof work resolves gate (a). An owner cannot responsibly authorize the render on this record: the authorizing act belongs to the owner/operator, but the listed technical prerequisites must be completed and reviewed first. The local attempt-002 proof cannot fill this gap: the handoff explicitly says it “does not manufacture an operator authorization” (handoff lines 18–20), and the proof report establishes only a supplied-plan loopback result (`hosted_requests: 0`, report line 21), not an actual production-path smoke.

**Required correction:** State the sequence explicitly: (1) finish/review/gate writer patch; (2) pass mapping-bound 8-object and non-empty-goal 12-object smokes on the selected production path with the named validations; (3) obtain explicit owner/operator authorization; (4) run the digest-bound render; (5) align and `verify_steps`; (6) authorize/complete pilot release; (7) create the calibration config/evaluation command. If local backend selection remains possible, place its separate owner decisions and localhost-adapter smoke before step 3, not as an afterthought.

### MAJOR — Step 8 incorrectly makes off-plan action policy a prerequisite for state-only rendering

**Location:** `doc/high_level_plans/research_execution_plan.md:533–534`, versus `task_plan.md:29` and `task_plan.md:17`.

**Offending text:** “Resolve the off-plan action-target policy before creating Qwen rows; **then render** the canonical missing-state request...”

**Why this is a consistency and gate-order failure:** The active task plan explicitly distinguishes the two operations: “Treat rendering and off-plan action selection as separate concerns; **the unresolved action policy does not block rendering**” (`task_plan.md:29`). Its hard boundary prohibits creating Qwen rows or inferring off-plan action targets without approval (`task_plan.md:17`), not rendering the frozen state-only request. The semicolon-plus-“then” construction therefore reinstates a dependency the task plan rejects. It needlessly holds up a 16,822-state technical render while still correctly forbidding Qwen-row creation.

**Required correction:** Split the action-policy and state-render branches. Keep “resolve policy before Qwen rows” as a separate gate; put rendering after its own technical preconditions and explicit operator authorization, not after the off-plan policy decision.
