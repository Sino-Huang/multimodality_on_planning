# Search-trace training and multimodal classical planning: research-gap audit

**Scope:** primary-source abstracts and methods available through 26 September 2026. Here a *plan* is the executed action sequence; a *plan/state CoT* narrates the chosen actions, their preconditions or resulting states; a *search trace* records deliberation **off the final plan** (candidate generation, expansions/backtracking, frontier/closed-set operations or priorities). A heuristic/policy learned *from* planner output is not a model trained to *emit* its search trace. “PDDL/IPC” below distinguishes formal PDDL benchmark domains from a custom maze/BlocksWorld simulator; a familiar domain name alone does not establish an IPC evaluation. An absence claim means **not identified in this survey**, not proof that no publication exists.

Direct counterexample to “nobody fine-tunes a pretrained LLM on A* traces” even outside planning. |

**Important cross-bucket distinctions.** A model that generates states **along an executed plan** is not thereby imitating a planner's explored-but-rejected states (PDDL-Instruct and Emami et al. vs. Searchformer). Training from plans produced *using* A*/BFWS does not imply training to emit A*/BFWS operations (PLANGPT and Gieselmann et al.). System-1.x **does** fine-tune a pretrained LLM on declared A* search in Blocksworld, so “only toy puzzles, only training from scratch, only one implicit algorithm” is **incorrect**; the defensible difference is breadth/representation/fidelity of IPC search, multimodal inputs and replay-based verification. In [Rossetti et al., ICAPS 2024, Related Work / §Comparison](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/download/31510/33670), a distinct **GNN** heuristic/policy baseline learns using BFWS-search-generated IPC data; that is further precedent for learning *from* planner internals, **not** an LLM/VLM trained to reproduce their verbalized operation sequence.

## Verdicts and defensible formulation

**(a) “Prior LLM planning training uses plan outputs, not search traces”: FALSE as an unrestricted statement; partially true only for the named plan-output lineage.** Plansformer, Pallagani, PLANGPT, Bohnet, Li, Belcamino and Gieselmann mainly optimize **actions/plans**, with PDDL-Instruct and Emami adding logical/state CoT ([§1](#1-language-model-training-on-classicalpddl-plans-and-adjacent-supervision)). But Searchformer and Dualformer train **A* operations** rather than only plans, and **System-1.x fine-tunes Mistral-7B on A* trajectories in Blocksworld** ([§2](#2-models-trained-on-search-computations-or-nearby-targets)). PDDL-Instruct also makes “final-plan-only” false even for PDDL training: logical CoT is not a frontier trace, but it is intermediate reasoning. ACPBench and ACPBench Hard are **evaluation**, not evidence of a trace-trained model.

**(b) “Nobody knows how an LLM/VLM performs learning a classical search trace”: FALSE; narrow to an untested *combination*.** Searchformer and Dualformer measure text A* trace learning on synthetic puzzles; System-1.x measures LoRA-tuned **pretrained LLM** A* tracing in custom Blocksworld/Maze (and BFS/DFS maze variants); Procedure Cloning measures BFS-computation imitation on mazes; Opedal et al. measure pretrained-LLM A* search-trace post-training for **deductive proofs** ([§2](#2-models-trained-on-search-computations-or-nearby-targets)). **[INFERENCE from papers examined]** The unestablished combination is *pretrained LLM/VLM fine-tuning to generate and replay-validate declared BFS/BFWS/greedy or weighted-A* (including h_add) search operations across multiple PDDL/IPC domains, comparing text, rendered-image and joint inputs*. The survey did **not** identify a full-search-operation VLM trained on visual PDDL/IPC states. System-1.x's Blocksworld A* imitation must be acknowledged directly, especially its limited candidate verbalizations; do **not** claim no previous LLM has learned search on Blocksworld or that all prior trace learners were trained from scratch.

**(c) “Multimodal planning is new”: FALSE broadly; the *intersection with planner-operation imitation* appears new in the surveyed sources.** Planimation renders PDDL states; ViPlan tests VLMs on visual PDDL Blocksworld; Visualizing Thought explores PDDL domains using diagrams; VPRL post-trains visual-planning models; and VLMFP **fine-tunes a VLM on image-conditioned stepwise action simulation in PDDL-formalized gridworlds** ([§4](#4-visualpddl-planning-and-multimodal-training-boundaries)). None of these cited methods trains a VLM to output the **off-plan priority/frontier/visited/search-operation** trace of declared BFS/BFWS/h_add planners over broad IPC domains; that qualified intersection, not images themselves, is the plausible contribution. Rendered-image-only vs text vs joint comparison and replay-based no-repair verification sharpen it further.

### Safe gap statement

> Previous work has demonstrated both direct plan supervision in PDDL domains and language-model imitation of symbolic search: in particular, Searchformer and Dualformer learn A* traces in maze/Sokoban, while System-1.x fine-tunes a pretrained LLM on A* search trajectories in maze and Blocksworld. In the primary sources examined here, we find no study that fine-tunes a pretrained **vision-language** model to emit **replay-verifiable operations of multiple declared classical search algorithms** across diverse PDDL/IPC domains using rendered observations, nor a matched text/image/joint-input comparison for that target. Our contribution should be framed as this **combination and its measured limits**, not as the first trace-trained LLM, the first search-supervised Blocksworld model, or the first multimodal planning system.
