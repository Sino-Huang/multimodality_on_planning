# Critique — 2026-08-31

## Scope

Reviewed only the title (`manuscript/iclr2026/iclr2026_conference.tex:12`) and abstract (`:55-57`). The remaining TeX is template material, not manuscript prose. The evidence boundary is the bounded BFS-only, text-state v6 corpus/release qualification—not a governed comparative efficacy result (`manuscript/content_brief.md:5,18-24`).

## Findings

- **MAJOR — the modality paragraph states an unimplemented design guarantee as a present fact.** “**Matched text-state, visual-state, and multimodal-state conditions preserve task semantics and the search-memory interface, allowing modality effects to be assessed independently of task content**” (`manuscript/iclr2026/iclr2026_conference.tex:56`) is written as an established property. There is no multimodal corpus and no completed modality matrix (`manuscript/content_brief.md:22-23`), while even the corpus contract remains a TODO (`:35`). This should be explicitly prospective (e.g., conditions *will be required/intended* to preserve these boundaries), not presented as an implemented control. As written, it quietly upgrades a desired matched-modality design into demonstrated experimental infrastructure.

- **MAJOR — present-tense evaluation language overstates the maturity of an unfrozen protocol.** “**Our evaluation is designed to distinguish**” and “**It reports budgeted episode success separately from operation validity, invariant compliance, and invalid-operation charges**” (`manuscript/iclr2026/iclr2026_conference.tex:56`) sounds like a settled evaluation instrument with reportable outputs. In fact, the evaluation protocol/results are “NOT STARTED” and its arms, estimands, split procedure, budget unit, seeds, uncertainty, and thresholds remain unspecified (`manuscript/content_brief.md:10,34`). “Comparative experiments are ongoing” at the paragraph’s end does not cure the mismatch: an ongoing experiment can still be governed by a frozen design, whereas this one is explicitly not. State that these are planned reporting criteria, or this registration abstract falsely conveys methodological completion.

- **MINOR — terminology is needlessly non-canonical at the point where the abstract defines the intervention.** “**text, visual, or multimodal state observations**” (`manuscript/iclr2026/iclr2026_conference.tex:56`) departs from the fixed names `text-state`, `visual-state`, and `multimodal-state` (`manuscript/CONTEXT.md:23-27`), although the later sentence uses them correctly. This forces readers to infer whether the first phrase denotes the same three declared modality contracts or looser observation types. Use the canonical labels consistently.

- **MINOR — the fourth arm is underspecified and obscures the planned causal contrast.** “**interactive data collection**” (`manuscript/iclr2026/iclr2026_conference.tex:56`) replaces the specified “process SFT plus DAgger” arm in the settled plan (`manuscript/content_brief.md:31`). “Interactive” could mean online evaluation, human collection, or DAgger; it is not a training arm definition. Either name DAgger or label the arm as a placeholder rather than disguising an unresolved intervention as a comparator.

## Claim discipline and suitability

The title, “**Can Vision-Language Models Learn to Execute Classical Search Algorithms?**” (`manuscript/iclr2026/iclr2026_conference.tex:12`), is a question rather than a result claim. Its plural algorithm and VLM framing is acceptable as intended study scope: the abstract says “**designed**” and ends “**Comparative experiments are ongoing; this temporary abstract will be updated with governed results**” (`:56`). It does not claim that IW/A*, visual, or multimodal corpora exist, and it does not manufacture numerical evidence. The operational-versus-structural/process distinction is clear and uses the governed terms appropriately: “**local operational competence**” versus “**full-episode structural/process competence**” (`:56`; cf. `manuscript/CONTEXT.md:7-19`). The runtime description also correctly retains external Search Memory and no silent repair. Thus this is potentially suitable as a candid temporary OpenReview registration abstract, but only if the two present-tense design claims above are made unambiguously prospective.

## Verdict

**BORDERLINE.** The single most damaging issue is the assertion that matched multimodal conditions already “preserve” semantic and Search Memory parity despite no implemented multimodal corpus or frozen modality protocol (`manuscript/iclr2026/iclr2026_conference.tex:56`).
