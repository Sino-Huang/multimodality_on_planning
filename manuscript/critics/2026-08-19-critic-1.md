# Critique: Confirmed Manuscript Design Artifacts

## Scope

Reviewed only `manuscript/content_brief.md`, `manuscript/CONTEXT.md`, and
`manuscript/writing_design_tree.md`, against the root `CONTEXT.md` / issue #38
evidence boundary. There are no drafted manuscript sections to review. The
root boundary is unambiguous: no Search Process Policy training run or efficacy
result exists.

## Findings

### CRITICAL — The artifacts simultaneously declare the manuscript design settled and leave the same foundational decisions open

`manuscript/writing_design_tree.md:14-17` says, “**Selected: an empirical
Search Process Policy paper drafted as a result-ready skeleton**” and “**Status:
SET**,” while `manuscript/content_brief.md:23-24` says “**Decide the manuscript
genre before defining the paper title, abstract, or section structure**” and
“**Decide whether the reusable historical infrastructure warrants ... a
protocol paper**.” This is not a harmless stale-status discrepancy: genre
determines whether an empirical title, a Results section, and a four-arm matrix
are appropriate at all. The tree also says its root decision “determines the
title, abstract, section structure, and permissible conclusion”
(`writing_design_tree.md:17`), precisely the items the brief claims remain
undecided.

The artifact therefore provides no authoritative answer to a future writer
about whether to frame unrun work as an empirical paper or a protocol. This
directly threatens the stated result boundary because the empirical framing can
be carried forward while its prerequisites remain absent. Resolve the conflict
in the confirmed source of truth before committing the design.

### MAJOR — The proposed title and “headline” conflict with the stated prohibition on implying an unrun result

The governing root decision says that, before gates complete, “**the title,
abstract, and conclusion must not imply demonstrated performance, modality
effects, or algorithmic success**” (`manuscript/writing_design_tree.md:15`).
Yet the very next title is “**Search Process Policies: Learning to Execute
Classical Search Algorithms with Vision-Language Models**”
(`writing_design_tree.md:21,40`), and the decision log fixes a “**headline
direct learning and execution of classical search algorithms**”
(`writing_design_tree.md:54`). “Learning to execute” is an accomplishment
claim in an empirical-paper title, not a neutral statement that the study will
ask whether learning occurs. This is especially indefensible next to “**No
current ... trained policy, or model-efficacy result exists**”
(`writing_design_tree.md:7`) and root `CONTEXT.md:7-9`.

The explicit `[TODO: result]` language protects the planned abstract
(`writing_design_tree.md:41`) but cannot undo a headline that precommits the
answer. A result-ready skeleton must either use a question/protocol framing
until a passed efficacy gate exists or defer the title; otherwise it gives a
writer a sanctioned way to claim success before the experiment runs.

### MAJOR — “Frozen evaluation” and the primary endpoint are asserted before the protocol contains the information required to freeze or interpret them

The design promises “**frozen staged evaluation**”
(`manuscript/writing_design_tree.md:41`), “**frozen budgets, whole-instance
splits**” (`writing_design_tree.md:45`), and a primary matrix whose “**joint
endpoints**” are “**full-episode budgeted success and Algorithm Invariant
validity**” (`writing_design_tree.md:46`). But its own log admits that “**detailed
claims, controls, metrics, and transfer conditions remain open**”
(`writing_design_tree.md:60`). The four arms are merely names — “**base,
operational-only SFT, offline process SFT, process SFT plus DAgger**”
(`writing_design_tree.md:45`) — with no specified data source, label/trace
construction, compute parity, checkpoint selection rule, random seeds, or
per-algorithm training allocation.

This is not a request for generic extra experiments. Without those frozen
choices, the planned comparison cannot distinguish the claimed effect of
process supervision from more data, online data collection, selection effort,
or an algorithm-specific curriculum. Before calling the evaluation frozen, the
artifact must lock an arm-by-arm data and compute contract, episode-budget
units, model-selection rule, instance-generation and split procedure, number
of independent training/evaluation runs, and the estimand plus uncertainty/test
for every algorithm-by-arm cell. Otherwise the advertised matrix is a
post-hoc-results template, not a preregistered empirical skeleton.

### MAJOR — The claimed “joint endpoints” collapse concepts the governing glossary explicitly separates

The root glossary defines “**validity**” as the runtime judgment that an
operation satisfies its contract and invariants and states that it “**is not by
itself episode success or competence**” (`CONTEXT.md:56-57`). Nevertheless,
the design repeatedly makes “**budgeted episode success and invariant
validity**” a “**joint**” primary endpoint (`manuscript/writing_design_tree.md:24,
46`). It never specifies whether validity is operation-level or episode-level,
whether an invalid operation makes the episode invalid, which denominator is
used after the required budget charge, or what decision rule combines the two
numbers. “Algorithm Invariant validity” is also not the canonical defined term:
the canonical term is an **Algorithm Invariant**, while **validity** applies to
a typed operation and its state effects.

This is a claim-evidence trap. An arm could produce nearly all valid operations
but fail every goal, or solve an episode after invalid operations consume budget;
neither situation has an unambiguous status in the proposed primary display.
The result table must separate operation-contract validity, invariant
compliance, invalid-operation rate and budget cost, and budgeted episode
success, then state in advance the gate rule that licenses a
structural/process-competence claim. That is required by the program's own
definition, not an optional reporting refinement.

### MINOR — The manuscript-local glossary silently broadens the canonical A* invariant

The root canonical definition gives the concrete A* condition “**A* frontier
order under h_max or landmark-count**” (`CONTEXT.md:37-39`). The local glossary
instead says “**A* frontier order under a specified heuristic**”
(`manuscript/CONTEXT.md:19`) while claiming it “**preserves the #38 canonical
planning-research language**” (`manuscript/CONTEXT.md:3`). “A specified
heuristic” permits materially different heuristics and hence a different
algorithm/invariant family from the ratified scope. Since A* variants are a
planned gate (`manuscript/writing_design_tree.md:33`), this is not cosmetic
terminology drift: it creates latitude to redefine the target after observing
results. Reuse the canonical qualifier verbatim or explicitly identify and
ratify any intended extension before evaluation.

### MINOR — The section architecture is inconsistent about whether the appendix exists

The planned architecture is listed as “**Abstract; Introduction; Related Work;
Search Process Policy; Experimental Design; Results; Discussion and
Limitations; Conclusion**” (`manuscript/content_brief.md:19`) and identically
at `manuscript/writing_design_tree.md:23`. Both artifacts then assign the full
evidence contract to “**The appendix**” (`content_brief.md:19`) and provide a
separate Appendix section with essential operation schemas, splits, statistics,
and receipts (`writing_design_tree.md:36,49`). Since the matrix’s validity and
stopping claims depend on those materials, omitting Appendix from the declared
architecture makes the evidence-bearing section look optional. Include it in
the fixed architecture or state explicitly that it is mandatory for any
empirical submission.

## Verdict

**REJECT — NOT READY TO COMMIT as a confirmed design artifact.** The artifact is
commendably explicit that no efficacy evidence exists, and it does not itself
report fabricated numerical results. However, the most damaging issue is the
CRITICAL contradiction between a settled result-ready empirical-paper decision
and a content brief that says the genre, title, and structure remain to be
decided. That contradiction leaves the claim boundary and the permitted paper
framing indeterminate before any experiment exists.
