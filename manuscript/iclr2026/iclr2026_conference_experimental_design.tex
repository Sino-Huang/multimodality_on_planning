\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:design}

% Paragraph purpose: State the comparative question, the gate logic, and the development-stage and seed boundaries.
Each algorithm-by-modality-by-condition cell asks whether a trained Search Process Policy exceeds an oracle-assisted validity control on full-episode budgeted success (Section~\ref{sec:policy}), under frozen protocols, a 336 GPU-hour cap, and prespecified gate receipts. Every executed stage is development-stage evidence. Panels are frozen and outcome-blind, the held-out manifest has never been accessed, and every learned cell uses one training seed (17) except two replicated cells (seeds 29 and 71 added, protocol frozen 2026-09-20).
% Evidence: seed replication per issue #127 (docs/experiments/expanded-study/issue-127-closeout.md).

% Paragraph purpose: Define conditions, recipe, and backbones.
Each cell holds the pretrained base, process SFT, and process SFT plus two DAgger iterations \citep{ross2011dagger} beside an exposure-matched continued-SFT control. SFT trains a fresh LoRA adapter \citep{hu2022lora} on 512 replay-verified records for one epoch (16 updates), except the BFWS gate checkpoint, which trained on the full 47,780-record corpus for two epochs. The backbone is Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct \citep{bai2025qwen3vl} (revision \texttt{0c351dd0}), with InternVL3.5-8B \citep{wang2025internvl35} as second backbone.

% Paragraph purpose: Define the references as oracle-assisted bounds and pin the random-valid sampler under each contract from the executed code.
Exact-classical runs the declared algorithm and bounds perfect decisions, random-valid proposes only runtime-valid operations, neither is a learned ability, and when a reference saturates a panel the learned-to-control gain there is unmeasurable rather than zero. Under the enumeration contract, random-valid reads the same runtime-built model input as the policy and, after filtering, draws one operation per decision uniformly at random with a seeded pseudo-random generator from the listed candidates, which are successor-candidate rows under the additive algorithms, non-duplicate evaluated successors with an admissible declared frontier position under BFWS, and unvisited successors under BFS, with a frontier-retire operation under BFWS and BFS when none remains. The sampled operation is submitted once through the same runtime path as a model output, with no retry and no repair. Under the choice-frontier contract the control is uniform menu choice of one offered frontier label per decision under a seeded generator, again with no retry or repair, so the two definitions share a name but not a distribution and each random-valid number is read under the contract of its row.

% Paragraph purpose: Fix splits, the main panel, budget, termination, separated measures, and the analysis contract.
Splits are by whole task instance with audited corpora (Data and Split Procedures appendix). The modality matrix evaluates 24 tasks from 12 domains under three observations, giving 288 episodes per arm. Budgets count decision calls with invalid operations charged. Learned adapters are capped at twice the exact-reference decision count, the base is priced at one call, and an episode ends at its first invalid emission and counts only after independent replay reproduces it. Contrasts use a paired whole-instance bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 1729, 95\% percentile) unless a protocol states otherwise (Statistics appendix), and transfer uses exact McNemar tests with Holm correction across 36 comparisons. At these sizes the intervals are descriptive bounds and cannot support broad superiority claims.

% Paragraph purpose: Define the two decision contracts and the shared identity audit, pointing to the schematic and the appendix table.
The two contracts are compared in Figure~\ref{fig:contracts} (Appendix) and Table~\ref{tab:design-contracts}. The choice-frontier contract has as its pre-registered pass condition the per-pair identity audit of random-valid against exact reference over 18 seed-17 pairs (\texttt{CHOICE\_SENSITIVE} on any divergence, \texttt{ZERO\_DECISION\_HEADROOM} otherwise, no re-runs), also run as a diagnostic on the enumeration contract's additive cells.

% Paragraph purpose: Define the #133 comparator zoo, its oracle-access asymmetry, primary metric, and claim unit.
The CPU-only, exploratory comparator zoo tests Pareto dominance over M1 (solve-versus-budget area over multipliers 1 to 2), M2 (expansion overhead), and M3 (plan cost) against five comparators under a fifteen-test Holm family, with \texttt{bfs-order}, novelty-first selection, and \texttt{worst-first} reading trusted $g$/$h$ scalars or PDDL atoms the model never sees (so not information-matched), and with all zoo metrics on the stored choice-frontier episodes post-hoc exploratory and non-significance licensing no equivalence claim.
