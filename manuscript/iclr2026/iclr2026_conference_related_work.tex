\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

% Paragraph purpose: Position against planning benchmarks and neural algorithm execution in one paragraph.
Planning benchmarks~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench,valmeekam2023critical,stein2025autoplanbench,kokel2026acpbenchhard} score plans or plan judgments rather than step-by-step execution of a declared algorithm such as A*~\citep{hart1968formal} or BFWS~\citep{lipovetzky2017bestfirst}, and trace-trained models~\citep{lehnert2024beyond,gandhi2024stream} and step imitators~\citep{velickovic2020neural,zaremba2014learning} lack typed operations and in-episode validation.

% Paragraph purpose: Position against model-in-the-loop search, step supervision, visual reasoning, and verification or constrained decoding in one paragraph.
In Tree of Thoughts~\citep{yao2023tree} and RAP~\citep{hao2023reasoning} a scaffold or UCT policy owns exploration, whereas our model issues the operations that determine it, and Scratchpads~\citep{nye2021show}, per-step labels~\citep{lightman2023lets}, Visual Sketchpad~\citep{hu2024visual}, and MVoT~\citep{li2025imagine} supervise or generate unchecked free-form steps and imagery rather than attributing control to an observation channel. Unlike repair loops~\citep{kambhampati2024llms,madaan2023selfrefine} and syntax-only constrained decoding~\citep{scholak2021picard,willard2023efficient}, our runtime never repairs and checks algorithmic invariants on every operation.

% Paragraph purpose: Position the audit against benchmark validity, shortcut analysis, and search-selector calibration.
Evaluation-validity work in planning and reasoning warns that common-sense tasks can confound planning with retrieval~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench}, and embodied suites such as BEHAVIOR-1K broaden task coverage~\citep{li2024behavior1k}. Research on multimodal training documents VQA models relying on language priors rather than image content, video-language evaluation reports selection bias toward answer positions~\citep{zhu2020overcoming,loginova2025addressing}, and LVLM chain-of-thought audits find that visual or format cues can influence answers without appearing in reasoning traces~\citep{balasubramanian2025closer}. Process supervision supplies feedback on intermediate reasoning steps~\citep{lightman2023lets}, but that training signal alone does not test whether a grounded action menu makes frontier choices identifiable. Prior text-game work treats access to valid actions as an evaluation handicap. Jericho lists world-change detection for identifying valid actions among its optional handicaps, and CALM reports that on half of its unseen games it is competitive with or better than models with access to ground-truth admissible actions~\citep{hausknecht2020interactive,yao2020keep}. The menu manipulations in this paper target this interface by testing whether a trained search policy follows menu surface rather than grounding its operation in state. This paper operationalizes the known admissible-action handicap in search-execution evaluation with a per-decision identity audit of random-valid against the exact reference on the additive best-first cells of the grounded-menu contract, then assesses the redesigned frontier selector against named algorithms rather than treating random-valid as a capability ceiling (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
