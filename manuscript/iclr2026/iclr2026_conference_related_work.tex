\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

% Paragraph purpose: Establish that language-model training for classical planning supervises the final plan or reasoning along a plan, not the planner's off-plan search, with planning benchmarks as evaluation context.
Language models trained for classical planning are typically supervised on plans, not on the search behind them. Plansformer~\citep{pallagani2022plansformer} and \citet{pallagani2023understanding} fine-tune pretrained language models to generate plans. PlanGPT trains a GPT-2-style model from scratch for each domain on planner-generated solution plans~\citep{rossetti2024learning}. Other work fine-tunes on optimal plans~\citep{bohnet2024exploring,li2024unlocking} or IPC~2023 plans with a validator reward~\citep{belcamino2026generalization}. \citet{belcamino2026generalization} also report that symbol anonymization and compact plan serialization cause significant drops despite preserving plan semantics, and that their plan-trained model reaches 0\% valid plans on two unseen domains. PDDL-Instruct tunes logical reasoning about action applicability and state transitions~\citep{verma2025teaching}, and \citet{emami2026close} interleave actions with resulting states. Both reason along a candidate plan, not over states a planner generated and discarded. Planning benchmarks score plans or plan judgments~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench,valmeekam2023critical,stein2025autoplanbench,kokel2026acpbenchhard}, and off-the-shelf language models improve at operational reasoning but not at structural enumeration~\citep{huang2026twodistinct}. We supervise the search itself, including states that never reach the plan.

% Paragraph purpose: Credit prior search-trace learning, name System-1.x as the closest work and state exactly how ours differs, then state the hedged gap and the claimed combination.
Search-trace training has precedent, including a pretrained language model fine-tuned on A* traces in Blocksworld. Procedure cloning imitates the BFS computations behind each expert action in synthetic mazes~\citep{yang2022chain}. Searchformer and Dualformer train transformers from scratch on A* traces of frontier and closed-set insertions in mazes and Sokoban~\citep{lehnert2024beyond,su2024dualformer}. Stream of Search trains a language model from scratch on linearized search in Countdown~\citep{gandhi2024stream}, and \citet{moon2024learning} fine-tune on improved search traces. \citet{opedal2026learning} fine-tune pretrained Llama models on A* execution traces for deductive proofs, not planning. The closest work, System-1.x~\citep{saha2024system1x}, LoRA-tunes a pretrained Mistral-7B on text A* traces in mazes and a custom Blocksworld, with BFS and DFS ablations on mazes only. In Blocksworld it verbalizes at most three valid and two invalid candidates per expansion, to fit its token budget. Our learner, a pretrained vision-language model, sees text, visual, or multimodal observations and is trained on BFS, BFWS, and additive best-first traces across PDDL domains. Under the enumeration contract, each expansion submits its complete candidate set, and a no-repair Trusted Search Runtime checks every emitted Typed Search Operation. We are not aware of a study that fine-tunes a pretrained vision-language model to emit replay-verifiable operations of several declared classical search algorithms across diverse PDDL domains, or that compares text, visual, and multimodal observations for this target. Our contribution is this combination and its measured limits.

% Paragraph purpose: Separate our setting from work that places a language model inside a search it does not run, and state the ownership point: the model issues every exploration-determining operation and the runtime never repairs.
Other work places a language model inside a search that it does not run. In Tree of Thoughts~\citep{yao2023tree} and RAP~\citep{hao2023reasoning}, a scaffold or upper-confidence policy owns expansion and selection. Thought of Search and AutoToS have the model write search-component code for an external search~\citep{katz2024thought,cao2024automating}, and \citet{correa2025classical} sample Python heuristics for greedy best-first search. LLM-First Search lets the model steer exploration with no declared algorithm~\citep{herr2025llmfirst}. Process supervision~\citep{lightman2023lets} scores free-form steps, repair loops revise the output~\citep{kambhampati2024llms,madaan2023selfrefine}, and constrained decoding enforces syntax only~\citep{scholak2021picard,willard2023efficient}. Our model instead issues every exploration-determining operation, and the runtime checks each against the algorithm's invariants and never repairs it.

% Paragraph purpose: Credit visual and multimodal planning, state that none of the cited systems emits a planner's search operations, and connect language-prior shortcut findings to our text-channel finding.
The closest multimodal planning work does not train a model to execute a declared search. ViPlan evaluates vision-language models as planners and as grounders for a symbolic planner on a visual PDDL Blocksworld~\citep{merler2025viplan}. Visualizing Thought generates diagrams inside inference-time graph-of-thought search on PDDL domains~\citep{borazjanizadeh2025visualizing}. VPRL post-trains vision models with reinforcement learning to plan through image sequences~\citep{xu2025visual}. VLMFP fine-tunes Qwen2-VL-7B to simulate action outcomes from grid-world images, and a second model writes PDDL for a planner~\citep{hao2025simulation}. None of these models emits a planner's frontier or visited-set operations. Models can also answer from language priors rather than the image~\citep{zhu2020overcoming}, or from cues absent from their reasoning trace~\citep{balasubramanian2025closer}. These findings bear on our result that text carries the learned signal under our setup (Section~\ref{sec:results-modality}).

% Paragraph purpose: Credit the controls the main text uses to established practice (the valid-action handicap for random-valid, matched-compute random baselines, learned node selection for the appendix choice-frontier study), and point to the appendix for the measurement audits.
Our controls adapt established practice. Random-valid chooses uniformly among the valid steps that the runtime supplies, so it is an oracle-assisted valid-action control, in the sense in which Jericho and CALM treat valid-action access as a disclosed handicap~\citep{hausknecht2020interactive,yao2020keep}. Scoring it under the same decision-counted budget as the model follows matched-compute random baselines in architecture search~\citep{li2019random,yang2020nasevaluation}. In the appendix choice-frontier study, the model performs learned node selection~\citep{orseau2021policyguided}, with per-decision label permutation against position bias~\citep{loginova2025addressing,zheng2024robust}. The appendix also reports measurement audits of both settings, which draw on heap tie-breaking~\citep{asai2016tiebreaking}, perturbation-based metric meta-evaluation~\citep{sai2021perturbation}, and a ScienceWorld positive control~\citep{wang2022scienceworld} (Appendices~\ref{sec:results-identity} and~\ref{sec:results-external}).
