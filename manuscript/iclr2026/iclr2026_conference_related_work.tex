\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

% Paragraph purpose: Position against planning benchmarks and neural algorithm execution in one paragraph.
Planning benchmarks~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench,valmeekam2023critical,stein2025autoplanbench,kokel2026acpbenchhard} score plans or plan judgments rather than step-by-step execution of a declared algorithm such as A*~\citep{hart1968formal} or BFWS~\citep{lipovetzky2017bestfirst}, and trace-trained models~\citep{lehnert2024beyond,gandhi2024stream} and step imitators~\citep{velickovic2020neural,zaremba2014learning} lack typed operations and in-episode validation. The choice-frontier contract is a learned node-selection policy in the sense of policy-guided heuristic search~\citep{orseau2021policyguided}, and the submission-order counterfactual isolates a heap tie-breaking effect of the kind studied for A* search~\citep{asai2016tiebreaking}.

% Paragraph purpose: Position against model-in-the-loop search, step supervision, visual reasoning, and verification or constrained decoding in one paragraph.
In Tree of Thoughts~\citep{yao2023tree} and RAP~\citep{hao2023reasoning} a scaffold or UCT policy owns exploration, whereas our model issues the operations that determine it, and Scratchpads~\citep{nye2021show}, per-step labels~\citep{lightman2023lets}, Visual Sketchpad~\citep{hu2024visual}, and MVoT~\citep{li2025imagine} supervise or generate unchecked free-form steps and imagery rather than attributing control to an observation channel. Unlike repair loops~\citep{kambhampati2024llms,madaan2023selfrefine} and syntax-only constrained decoding~\citep{scholak2021picard,willard2023efficient}, our runtime never repairs and checks algorithmic invariants on every operation.

% Paragraph purpose: Position the audit against benchmark validity, shortcut analysis, search-selector calibration, and baseline practice.
Evaluation-validity work in planning and reasoning warns that common-sense tasks can confound planning with retrieval~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench}, and embodied suites such as BEHAVIOR-1K broaden task coverage~\citep{li2024behavior1k}. Multimodal-training research documents VQA models relying on language priors rather than image content~\citep{zhu2020overcoming}, and video-language evaluation reports answer-position selection bias~\citep{loginova2025addressing}. Label-position selection bias is documented for LLM multiple-choice selectors~\citep{zheng2024robust}, and the fixed-versus-random-position distractor test is the corresponding unrun diagnostic for menu-based search. LVLM chain-of-thought audits find visual or format cues can influence answers without appearing in reasoning traces~\citep{balasubramanian2025closer}. Process supervision supplies intermediate-step feedback~\citep{lightman2023lets}, but that signal alone does not test whether a grounded action menu makes frontier choices identifiable. Random search under a matched evaluation budget is the standard baseline in architecture search~\citep{li2019random}. Text-game work treats valid-action access as an evaluation handicap. Jericho lists world-change detection for identifying valid actions among its optional handicaps, and CALM reports that on half of its unseen games it is competitive with or better than models with access to ground-truth admissible actions~\citep{hausknecht2020interactive,yao2020keep}. ScienceWorld~\citep{wang2022scienceworld} is a published sequential-choice interface that likewise exposes a per-step choice among valid actions, so a random-valid control there registers choice. Our identity audit checks this property per decision, and the complete-set submission contract where it fails was not found among the surveyed published interfaces. The menu manipulations probe this interface, and only the unrun fixed-versus-random-position variant would separate surface-following from state grounding. This paper operationalizes the known admissible-action handicap in search-execution evaluation with a per-decision identity audit that returns both verdicts on one runtime and anchors the validated choice measurement of Section~\ref{sec:results}.
