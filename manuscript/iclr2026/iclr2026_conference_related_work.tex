\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

% Paragraph purpose: State the execution-evaluation position against planning benchmarks, trace-trained models, and neural algorithm execution, absorbing the operational-versus-structural split.
We evaluate step-by-step execution of declared algorithms such as A*~\citep{hart1968formal} or BFWS~\citep{lipovetzky2017bestfirst}, not plans. Planning benchmarks~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench,valmeekam2023critical,stein2025autoplanbench,kokel2026acpbenchhard} score plans or plan judgments, and off-the-shelf language models improve at operational reasoning but not at structural enumeration~\citep{huang2026twodistinct}. Trace-trained models~\citep{lehnert2024beyond,gandhi2024stream} and step imitators~\citep{velickovic2020neural,zaremba2014learning} likewise lack typed operations and in-episode validation. Our choice-frontier contract, where the model chooses which frontier state to expand, is a learned node-selection policy~\citep{orseau2021policyguided}. The submission-order counterfactual isolates a heap tie-breaking effect of the kind studied for A* search~\citep{asai2016tiebreaking}.

% Paragraph purpose: State the control-attribution position against model-in-the-loop search, step supervision, visual reasoning, and verification or constrained decoding, with the no-repair runtime as our contrast.
Our model issues the operations that determine exploration, whereas in Tree of Thoughts~\citep{yao2023tree} and Reasoning via Planning (RAP)~\citep{hao2023reasoning} a scaffold or upper-confidence policy owns them. Scratchpads~\citep{nye2021show}, per-step labels~\citep{lightman2023lets}, Visual Sketchpad~\citep{hu2024visual}, and Multimodal Visualization-of-Thought (MVoT)~\citep{li2025imagine} supervise or generate unchecked free-form steps and imagery. Unlike repair loops~\citep{kambhampati2024llms,madaan2023selfrefine} and syntax-only constrained decoding~\citep{scholak2021picard,willard2023efficient}, our runtime never repairs and checks algorithmic invariants on every operation.

% Paragraph purpose: Position the audit within evaluation-validity and shortcut analysis, locating those confounds in the model or the data and ours in the runtime, and state that the per-decision label permutation is the standard mitigation.
Shortcut work locates the confound in the model or the data, and ours lives in the runtime. Evaluation-validity work warns that common-sense tasks can confound planning with retrieval~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench}, and embodied suites extend evaluation coverage~\citep{li2024behavior1k}. Multimodal-training research documents visual question answering (VQA) models relying on language priors rather than image content~\citep{zhu2020overcoming}, and video-language evaluation reports answer-position selection bias~\citep{loginova2025addressing}. Label-position selection bias is documented for LLM multiple-choice selectors~\citep{zheng2024robust}, and large vision-language model (LVLM) chain-of-thought audits find visual or format cues can influence answers without appearing in reasoning traces~\citep{balasubramanian2025closer}. Our choice-frontier contract instead permutes candidate labels per decision, the standard mitigation rather than a contribution. Process supervision~\citep{lightman2023lets} supplies intermediate-step feedback but does not test whether a grounded action menu makes frontier choices identifiable.

% Paragraph purpose: Position the controls and the audit within matched-budget baseline practice and published interface practice, crediting the budget principle and stating both novelties without overclaiming.
Matched-budget random baselines and valid-action handicaps are known, and we apply them. Random search at matched compute is the standard architecture-search baseline~\citep{li2019random}, and relative-to-random reporting removes an expertly engineered search space's advantage~\citep{yang2020nasevaluation}, so we apply this matched-baseline principle as a budget check and add no new budget principle. Jericho and the Contextual Action Language Model (CALM) treat valid-action access as a disclosed handicap~\citep{hausknecht2020interactive,yao2020keep}, and no per-decision test checks whether uniform choice already reproduces the reference. ScienceWorld~\citep{wang2022scienceworld} exposes per-step choice among valid actions and is a positive control, since random-valid registers choice there. This paper operationalizes the known admissible-action handicap in search-execution evaluation with a per-decision identity audit that returns both verdicts on one runtime and admits the validated choice measurement of Section~\ref{sec:results}. We also validate the success measure against selectors of known order before scoring any policy, and the rungs read privileged information, so this checks the measurement, not a policy.
