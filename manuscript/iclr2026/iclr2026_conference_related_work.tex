\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

% Paragraph purpose: State the execution-evaluation position against planning benchmarks and neural algorithm execution.
We evaluate step-by-step execution of declared algorithms such as A*~\citep{hart1968formal} or BFWS~\citep{lipovetzky2017bestfirst}. Planning benchmarks~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench,valmeekam2023critical,stein2025autoplanbench,kokel2026acpbenchhard} instead score plans or plan judgments. Trace-trained models~\citep{lehnert2024beyond,gandhi2024stream} and step imitators~\citep{velickovic2020neural,zaremba2014learning} likewise lack typed operations and in-episode validation. Our choice-frontier contract, where the model chooses which frontier state to expand, is a learned node-selection policy~\citep{orseau2021policyguided}. The submission-order counterfactual isolates a heap tie-breaking effect of the kind studied for A* search~\citep{asai2016tiebreaking}.

% Paragraph purpose: State the control-attribution position against model-in-the-loop search, step supervision, visual reasoning, and verification or constrained decoding.
Our model issues the operations that determine exploration, whereas in Tree of Thoughts~\citep{yao2023tree} and Reasoning via Planning (RAP)~\citep{hao2023reasoning} a scaffold or upper confidence bounds applied to trees (UCT) policy owns it. Scratchpads~\citep{nye2021show}, per-step labels~\citep{lightman2023lets}, Visual Sketchpad~\citep{hu2024visual}, and Multimodal Visualization-of-Thought (MVoT)~\citep{li2025imagine} supervise or generate unchecked free-form steps and imagery rather than attributing control to an observation channel. Unlike repair loops~\citep{kambhampati2024llms,madaan2023selfrefine} and syntax-only constrained decoding~\citep{scholak2021picard,willard2023efficient}, our runtime never repairs and checks algorithmic invariants on every operation.

% Paragraph purpose: Position the audit within evaluation-validity work and shortcut analysis.
Our audit asks whether success reflects choice or only validity. Evaluation-validity work warns that common-sense tasks can confound planning with retrieval~\citep{valmeekam2023planbench}, and embodied suites such as BEHAVIOR-1K broaden task coverage~\citep{li2024behavior1k}. Multimodal-training research documents visual question answering (VQA) models relying on language priors rather than image content~\citep{zhu2020overcoming}, and video-language evaluation reports answer-position selection bias~\citep{loginova2025addressing}. Label-position selection bias is documented for LLM multiple-choice selectors~\citep{zheng2024robust}, and large vision-language model (LVLM) chain-of-thought audits find visual or format cues can influence answers without appearing in reasoning traces~\citep{balasubramanian2025closer}. Our manipulations of the action menu are the corresponding diagnostic, and only the unrun test that compares fixed with random distractor positions would separate surface-following from state grounding. Process supervision supplies intermediate-step feedback~\citep{lightman2023lets}, but that signal alone does not test whether a grounded action menu makes frontier choices identifiable.

% Paragraph purpose: Position the controls and the audit within matched-budget baseline practice and published interface practice.
Our random-valid control follows baseline practice, and our audit extends interface checks to individual decisions. Matched-budget random search is the standard architecture-search baseline~\citep{li2019random}, and text-game work treats valid-action access as an evaluation handicap. Jericho lists world-change detection for identifying valid actions among its optional handicaps, and the Contextual Action Language Model (CALM) reports it matches or beats models given ground-truth admissible actions on half of its unseen games~\citep{hausknecht2020interactive,yao2020keep}. ScienceWorld~\citep{wang2022scienceworld} likewise exposes a per-step choice among valid actions, so a random-valid control there registers choice. The identity audit checks this property per decision, and no surveyed published interface implements the enumeration contract on which it fails. This paper operationalizes the known admissible-action handicap in search-execution evaluation with a per-decision identity audit that returns both verdicts on one runtime and anchors the validated choice measurement of Section~\ref{sec:results}.
