\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

% Paragraph purpose: State the frozen protocol discipline, seed policy, and episode-level replay verification via the replay-determinative interface.
Every stage of the study runs under a frozen outcome-blind protocol.
Development panels and held-out manifests are frozen before any model outcome.
Pre-registered gates close each stage with a recorded receipt (\textsc{pass},
\textsc{valid\_stop}, \textsc{invalid}, or \textsc{ancestor\_stop}), and no gate was revised after
observing results.  Training seeds are frozen pre-launch: seed~17 for every
cell after the historical pilot, with seeds~29 and~71 added for the two
prospective replication cells.  The follow-up windows likewise train one
seed-17 run per cell unless a protocol states otherwise, and stress tests R1,
R2, and R4 run on frozen checkpoints without retraining.  No seed was
dropped after outcomes.  The
replay-determinative interface makes every episode independently verifiable
from its raw emissions alone.  The policy emits Typed Search Operations with
explicit operands, and the Trusted Search Runtime validates and applies each
operation without filling omitted choices or repairing outputs. Every episode
record (model and control) across the seven branches, the observation-corruption
suite (360 new + 144 reused bindings), and the seed-replication amendment (534 episodes)
was independently replayed with zero missingness. The 1,152 expanded-baseline
bindings include 576 model episodes and 576 CPU control/reference episodes.

% Paragraph purpose: Give the replay receipts for the follow-up branches, window by window.
Every follow-up branch was replayed with the same discipline and zero
missingness.  The native arms replayed 216/216 new bindings plus
72/72 reused comparators.  Stress tests R1, R2, and R4
replayed 170/170 episodes, and the second-backbone v3 cell (stress test R3)
replayed 72/72 model episodes plus 72/72 comparators.  The choice-frontier
evaluation replayed 144/144 episodes, and its 58 preparation episodes
were re-derived bit-exact.  The comparator zoo replayed 432/432
bindings, 342 of them new CPU episodes.

% Paragraph purpose: Identify the released artifacts and their exact counts.
Two process corpora supply the training data.  The BFS text-state corpus
contains 90 replay-verified traces and 25,109 process rows.  The BFWS corpus
covers 105 tasks and 69,019 replay-verified teacher decisions, projecting to a
47,780-train / 21,239-dev split.  Both passed no-overlap, no-conflict,
live-parity, and token-budget audits before use.  Per-cell training
configurations, evaluation manifests, LoRA adapter weights with checkpoint
digests, and the synthesis claims inventory are recorded for each executed
stage.

% Paragraph purpose: State the compute envelope per ledger and the statistical procedures.
The program consumed 68.5244 of a capped 336 GPU-hours on
$2\times$NVIDIA A100 80\,GB, with a per-branch ledger that retains failed and
cutoff attempts at their recorded cost.  The follow-up windows ran on separate
ledgers with their own caps, and their spend is not added to the program
total.  The native-arms window spent 3.2086 of 12 GPU-hours, the R1-R4
follow-up window 7.7998 of 36, and the choice-frontier window 1.069 of 12.  The
choice-frontier corpus derivation and controls ran on CPU, and the comparator
zoo ran on CPU only.  Paired contrasts use whole-instance
bootstrap with 10,000 resamples at seed~1729 and 95\% percentile intervals,
except where a follow-up protocol fixes its own seed (Statistics appendix).
Transfer comparisons use exact McNemar tests with Holm correction across the
36 adapter--base pairs.  Strata with fewer than 8 instances are reported as
descriptive only.  All seeds, panel memberships, corruption parameters, and
frozen protocols are stated in the evaluation manifests.
