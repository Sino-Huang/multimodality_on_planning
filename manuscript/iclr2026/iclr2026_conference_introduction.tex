\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

% Paragraph purpose: Motivate training search execution from the prior two-competency diagnosis, state the policy/runtime mechanism and scope, and fix the seed and held-out boundaries.
On classical planning probes \citep{valmeekam2023critical,stein2025autoplanbench,kokel2026acpbenchhard}, off-the-shelf language models improve at operational reasoning with scale and prompting but not at structural enumeration \citep{huang2026twodistinct}, leaving open whether a model can be trained to sustain a declared search procedure over a budgeted episode. We train a Search Process Policy (Section~\ref{sec:policy}) to emit Typed Search Operations that a no-repair Trusted Search Runtime validates against Algorithm Invariants, with every exploration-determining operand emitted so that replay reproduces the trace, and we measure validity and success separately from advantage over an oracle-assisted random-valid control. The algorithms are BFS, complete unpruned width-based best-first search (BFWS), and weighted A* (weight 3) and greedy best-first over $h_{\rm add}$, under text, visual, and multimodal observations (Section~\ref{sec:design}). All cells are single-seed except two replication cells (seeds 17/29/71, descriptive $n{=}3$), and the held-out final primary evaluation has not been executed, so no final comparative efficacy finding exists.

Under the enumeration contract (Section~\ref{sec:results}), a per-decision identity audit finds random-valid and exact reference decision- and expansion-identical on 48/48 pairs of the additive best-first cells, because an expansion submits its complete candidate set and heap order ignores submission order, so choice quality is unmeasurable there and saturating random-valid entries measure operation validity, while the BFS and width-based cells register choice and distractor injection shows the policy does not reject inapplicable menu entries. One positive learned contrast exists, on a 15-task text-state BFWS development panel (one fixed-seed run 0.933, random-valid 0.467, base 0.0, exact 1.0). On the 24-task baseline, process SFT solved 125 of 288, all in additive cells, against 240 for random-valid (96 in the choice-registering BFS/BFWS cells), 0 for the base, and 288 for exact. Over 2,910 replayed episodes the dominant learned failure (229/407) is an invariant rejection of a parsed, applicable operation, while random-valid fails only late on budget exhaustion. Text shuffling costs $-0.944$ (text) and $-0.722$ (multimodal) against at most $-0.111$ for visual corruption. An InternVL3.5-8B greedy cell reaches 31/36 (base 0/36, random-valid solves every panel task with 24/24 control entries per modality, two per task), replicating contract fluency, with its earlier 0/36 BFS result consistent with a choice-requirement difference rather than a backbone null. DAgger tied exposure-matched continued SFT (1/72 each), model-generated successors collapsed success (45/45 to 1/45), and no transfer comparison survived Holm correction (0/36).

% Paragraph purpose: Present the audit-driven choice-frontier redesign, its identity gate as a sanity check, the first measurement, and the comparator-zoo calibration with its non-equivalence boundary.
The redesign (Figure~\ref{fig:contracts}) makes the policy select which runtime-owned frontier state to expand under a budget of 2$\times$ the additive exact-reference expansion count. Its pre-registered identity audit, a sanity check that holds by construction whenever a menu offers two or more states, passes on 9/9 tasks (18 cells, control sequences coincide across the two algorithms), and random-valid and exact separate ($-0.644$, frozen 95\% CI $[-0.856, -0.422]$, post-hoc task-clustered $[-0.889, -0.333]$). The adapter solves 7/18 cells with zero invalid operations, a contract-fluency gain of $+0.389$ $[+0.167, +0.611]$ over the base, but its contrast against random-valid is $+0.033$ $[-0.033, +0.133]$, not material, with policy-level resolution resting on one discriminative task. An exploratory comparator zoo finds no positive learned Pareto dominance under a fifteen-test Holm family. On solve-versus-budget AUC the adapter (.1042) lies between \texttt{bfs-order} (.0694) and novelty-first selection (.1250) with overlapping intervals, while random-valid (.1389) is the uniform-choice reference and selectors read privileged information the model does not see. With no pre-registered margin, non-significance is neither equivalence nor a generalized negative capability claim.

This paper makes three contributions.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,parsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{A measurability audit for grounded-menu search evaluation.} Submission-order invariance makes random-valid decision-identical to the reference on the additive enumeration-contract cells (48/48 pairs), and the same check finds 18/18 divergent pairs under the choice-frontier contract. The audit is a per-decision identity check, plus a menu-manipulation probe whose discriminating variant (fixed versus random distractor position) remains unrun.
% Evidence: outputs/native-arms/v1/identity-audit.json pairs_identical 48; outputs/choice-frontier/v1/evaluation/identity-audit.json pairs_divergent 18 (review 18, finding 49).
    \item \textbf{An audit-driven choice-frontier contract.} On a replay-determinative, no-repair policy/runtime interface, the policy chooses which frontier state to expand under a binding budget. Its pre-registered identity gate (a by-construction sanity check) passes on 9/9 tasks and random-valid and exact separate, with policy-level resolution resting on one discriminative task.
    \item \textbf{A development-stage map with its negative boundaries.} The choice-frontier measurement shows contract fluency without demonstrated choice quality beyond the named references or positive learned Pareto dominance, and the enumeration contract has one positive contrast on the 15-task width-based panel. The dominant learned failure (229/407) is an invariant rejection of a parsed, applicable operation. Text shuffling outweighs visual corruption, DAgger adds no advantage, model-generated successors collapse goal success, and transfer leaves no comparison surviving correction, all development-stage rather than final efficacy.
\end{itemize}
